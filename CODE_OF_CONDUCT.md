# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience or
  language. Arabic and English are both welcome in issues and discussions.
- Critique ideas, data and code, not people. Assume good faith.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing, or publishing other people's
  private information.
- Results here are synthetic and uncalibrated: do not present them as real forecasts about
  Jeddah, its residents or its authorities.

## Scope

It applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, the project page) and when
someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at **tariq.lutf@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments or contributions, or temporarily or
permanently ban a contributor, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant
2.1.
