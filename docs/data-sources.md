# Data sources

All TrafficTwin Jeddah results are **synthetic demand, uncalibrated** until M8 says otherwise. The sources below feed the network (M1) and the synthetic population (M2); none of them is observed traffic.

Repository policy: `data/` is gitignored. Only `data/demo/`, `data/osm/fixes/` and small, licence-compatible public tables in `data/census/` (each with a `README.md` stating the licence) are committed. Everything else is described here and fetched by scripts, not stored.

Access date for every entry: **2026-10-15** unless stated. Status values: used / planned / not found / unverified. "Verified" means checked on the web on the access date; "inference" marks our own reasoning.

**Data-quality flag (signals).** 338 signal nodes collapsing to 72 TLS programs for a 3.7 M city is very likely far below the real count (inference; no official signal inventory found). Unmapped signals become priority or uncontrolled junctions in SUMO, which overstates capacity at major intersections. Mitigation: add reviewed signals to `data/osm/fixes/` (and upstream to OSM where verifiable), use `netconvert --tls.guess` with documented thresholds as a registered assumption, and list the signal count in `docs/assumptions.md`.

**Note 1: census figure.** `3,712,917` is GASTAT's 2022 count for the **place** "Jiddah" (the city), not the governorate. The **Jeddah governorate** total is `3,751,722` (the three listed places, Jiddah + Thuwal 26,957 + Dhahban 9,436, sum to 3,749,310, so 2,412 people live outside named places). Both are verified only via secondary sources citing GASTAT (citypopulation.de; Mawdoo3 for 3,751,722). The primary GASTAT census portal (portal.saudicensus.sa) did not resolve from this machine on 2026-10-15, so the primary table is **unverified**. Recommendation (inference): use 3,712,917 as the control total, because Thuwal (KAUST) and Dhahban lie north of our polygon's 21.90 N limit. Re-check against the GASTAT table before committing it to `data/census/`. Other figures seen on citypopulation.de for the place: 2,263,979 male / 1,448,938 female; 1,698,010 Saudi / 2,014,907 non-Saudi; both splits sum to 3,712,917 (unverified at primary source).

**Note 2: GTFS.** No Jeddah or Saudi GTFS feed exists in the Mobility Database catalogue (`feeds_v2.csv`, checked 2026-10-15: zero Saudi feeds; one row tagged SA is a Turkish operator) or on Transitland (no agencies match "jeddah"). JTC press releases and the "Jeddah Buses" app mention GPS and dashboard data but no open feed. TGA and the Public Transport Authority: no feed found (unverified). Fallback (PROJECT.md s. 13): OSM route relations plus a hand-written GTFS-like YAML per line with `source:`.

## Not found / to verify

- **District-level (hay) population: not found.** GASTAT publishes 2022 census results to governorate and place level; no per-hay table was found. open.data.gov.sa rejected automated requests ("Request Rejected" WAF page), so its catalogue is **unverified**, not absent. No Jeddah Municipality (Amanah) open-data portal or district shapefile was found. Fallback (PROJECT.md s. 13): distribute the census total over OSM districts by residential building footprint area (optionally footprint area x levels), registered in `docs/assumptions.md`.
- **Official district boundaries: not found.** A third-party dataset (Tabaqat `geocoding-cng` on Source Cooperative, https://source.coop/tabaqat/geocoding-cng) lists 208 Jeddah districts with geometries and no population; its licence and boundary provenance are not stated, so do not use it until both are known. Use OSM admin_level=10.
- **Household travel survey (trips per person, mode share): not found for Jeddah.** A 2025 framework paper (MDPI Vehicles, DOI 10.3390/vehicles8060139) states Saudi Arabia lacks a national household travel survey; published surveys found are Tabuk (1,220 households) and Dammam (school travel). Use documented assumptions exposed in the sweep tool.
- **Official GTFS for JTC/SAPTCO: not found** (note 2). A direct request to JTC is the route to one.
- **Traffic signal inventory: not found** (see the signals flag above).
- **MoE school locations: unverified** (a commercial catalogue exists; not usable).
- **GASTAT labour table URL at governorate level: not found**; region level only.
- **Primary GASTAT census table: unverified** (portal unreachable on the access date).

## Licence obligations

- **OpenStreetMap (ODbL 1.0).** Show "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors" with a link to https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright in `NOTICE`, the README, the viewer map and any published figure. Network files, district GeoJSON, building layers and fix lists are derived databases: if we publicly share them (e.g. `data/demo/*.net.xml`), they must be offered under ODbL (share-alike), and the changes we made must be documented. Simulation outputs and images are Produced Works: attribution required, the works themselves may stay Apache-2.0.
- **Eclipse SUMO (EPL-2.0).** Installed from the PyPI wheel, not vendored; nothing to redistribute. If SUMO source is ever modified or vendored, those files stay EPL-2.0 and their source must be made available.
- **GASTAT.** Credit GASTAT as the official source on every table and chart that uses its figures; mark any modification (e.g. "derived by TrafficTwin from GASTAT Census 2022"); never present derived tables as official statistics; do not use GASTAT logos. Check each indicator for a specific policy before committing it to `data/census/`.
- **Python dependencies.** `adhanpy` and `hijridate` are MIT (compatible with Apache-2.0). Avoid `adhan` (LGPL-3.0, unmaintained).
- **Commercial traffic APIs** (Google, TomTom, HERE): not used. Their terms forbid redistribution; any future integration is an adapter in `sim/adapters/` with no data committed.
- **Press and thesis sources:** facts only, cited by URL; no text or figures copied.
