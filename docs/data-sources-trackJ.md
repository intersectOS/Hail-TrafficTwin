## 1. Obhur crossing plans

**Verdict: yes.** A road and metro bridge across Sharm Obhur, linking South and North Obhur, has been planned, prequalified, tendered and studied since 2009. It was **never awarded or built**. No source dated 2023-2026 reports an award, a financial close or construction.

**Where on the creek.** No source publishes coordinates.
- *Inference, low-medium confidence:* the 2020 alignment (Madinah Road, then King Abdulaziz Road, then Obhur Corniche, then the creek, then north Prince Abdullah Al-Faisal Road) approaches from the **east/south-east through South Obhur**. That points to a **mid-to-inner (eastern) creek crossing**, not the mouth near Jeddah Tower.
- The SYSTRA "360 m bay" width fits the 350-380 m crossing length quoted in 2014-2019.
- The 2 km figure is the whole structure including approaches.
- Because the 2021+ JDA study re-opened 5 alignment options, treat the location as **open**.
- **Recommendation for the model:** test 2-3 candidate sites (mid-creek on the King Abdulaziz Road / Prince Abdullah Al-Faisal axis as the base case; mouth; head). Label all of them "assumption".

**Dimension conflicts:** the length is 350 m, 380 m or 2 km depending on whether the source counts only the water crossing (about 360 m bay) or the whole structure. All sources agree on 74-74.5 m width and 8 lanes (4+4) plus metro.

**Jeddah Tower / Jeddah Economic City access:** nothing reliable was found on dedicated external road access.
- Verified: JEC internal roads and landscaping were "90% done", with a pedestrian/green emphasis ([Arab News, 24 Jun 2021](https://www.arabnews.com/business/jeddah-economic-city-90-of-road-landscaping-work-done-1882861)).
- *Inference:* the JDA Northern Obhur Road (row 9) is the corridor that would serve the north bank, JEC included. No source states this.

## 2. Bridge cost ranges

- 1 USD/ft² = 10.764 USD/m².
- USD M per lane-km = USD/m² × W × 1000 / 1e6, where **W = 5.0 m gross deck per lane**. This is an *assumption*: a 3.65 m lane plus a share of shoulders, median, barriers and walkways. The Obhur design is 74 m / 8 lanes = 9.25 m because it also carries the metro.
- AED to USD at 3.6725 (peg).
- Costs are nominal in the year shown; no inflation adjustment.

**Type adjustment (from the sources above):**
- **Fixed low** bridges sit at the bottom of the girder ranges (about USD 7-15M per lane-km; Texas, Caltrans low end).
- **Fixed high/navigable** bridges sit at USD 15-35M per lane-km (Dubai Islands, FHWA average, Gulf arches).
- **Bascule/movable** bridges cost about **1.4-1.6×** a fixed high bridge in capital or life-cycle cost (Cortez, Hampton), and add a lift-delay event for the simulation.
- FDOT notes movable, arch and cable-stayed types have no bid-history averages and must be estimated from first principles ([FDOT SDG ch. 11, 2002](https://www.fdot.gov/docs/default-source/structures/Manuals/LRFDSDG2002AugChap11.pdf)).
- Not found: a World Bank ROCKS bridge rate, or a published Saudi road-bridge contract value with length and lanes.

## 3. Navigation

- The creek is about 9 km long ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obhur_Creek)). Its hydrography is in El-Rayis & Eid (1997, J. KAU Marine Sci. 8:29-45); the abstract has no width or depth figures.
- Marinas inside the creek:
  - **Red Sea Marina**: 300 berths, yachts up to 60 m. Source is a travel-guide claim; confirm with the operator.
  - **Jeddah Yachts Marina**: North Obhur, founded 1996 (directory listing).
  - Jeddah Yacht Club & Marina is on the central Corniche, **not** in Obhur.
- No vessel-traffic counts were found.
- **Clearance:** no published navigation clearance requirement for an Obhur bridge was found.
  - The "51 m maximum clearance" in MEED 2018 matches SYSTRA's "51 m at its peak" for the bow-string arch. *Inference:* it is the arch crown height, not the navigation air draft.
  - The 2009 study included "navigation permits" (row 1) and the 2021 op-ed raises large-boat passage (row 11).
  - Regional benchmarks: Dubai Islands bridge 18.5 m over a 75 m channel; Infinity Bridge 15.5 m; US fixed bridges over intracoastal waterways 65 ft (19.8 m).
  - **Model fallback (assumption):** 18-20 m clearance over a 75 m channel at the bridge, with no lifts. A bascule variant should add an opening event that closes the deck periodically, with frequency and duration as assumptions.
