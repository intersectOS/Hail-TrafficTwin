A traffic digital twin of Jeddah, built in the open

TrafficTwin simulates a full 24-hour day for the whole city on the real OpenStreetMap road network, with demand from a synthetic population. It answers policy questions (remote work, staggered school hours, Ramadan, bus lines, road pricing, Loop tunnels), injects events into microscopic focus windows, and ranks emergency response plans for a human to approve. Built on Eclipse SUMO, Python and deck.gl; Apache-2.0.

Where would a Loop tunnel help Jeddah most?

The model sited candidate Boring-Company-style Loop corridors, simulated each on the city model, and swept operating policies under measured (Las Vegas) capacity.

  1. Corridor C1, Al-Salamah to King Abdulaziz University (17.9 km, 8 stations) gives the most relief per dollar among the candidates.
  2. About 63,000 vehicle-hours of road delay saved per day (mean of two seeds; noise band 23,000 to 103,000), AM-peak road delay -2.9 %.
  3. 28,820 passengers per day as a shuttle capped at measured capacity, for an estimated USD 268 to 714 M capex; 70 % of riders would still be slower than by car under measured station waits.

10 % sample, seed 1 and 2, gridlock-affected baseline; shift share is an assumption (no mode-choice model). Full method and caveats: docs/loop.md.

Architecture

One engine at two resolutions. Tier A runs all of Jeddah mesoscopically for policy questions; Tier B cuts a 3-25 km² focus window out of it and runs every vehicle for events and the strategist. Layers only talk through run directories.

Policies change the population or the network before a run; events act through TraCI during a run. The AI recommends; a human approves high-impact actions. Full diagram: docs/architecture.md.

Quickstart

macOS (Apple silicon) or Linux with uv, Node 22+, make and curl. The demo window runs on any recent laptop; the city adds a 243 MB download and peaks at about 3 GB of RAM (measured on an 18 GB M3 Pro).

git clone https://github.com/__REPO__.git TrafficTwin && cd TrafficTwin
make demo CITY=0     # demo window only: setup + demo runs, then the viewer on http://localhost:5173
make demo            # adds the Jeddah city network and a one-hour city run (about 7 min first time)
make test            # ruff, pytest on the demo window, web typecheck

Everything else (24 h city baseline, policies, events, strategist, evacuation, Loop, calibration, films) is in the README.
