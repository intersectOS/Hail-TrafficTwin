# Track D — Jeddah Loop tunnels: siting and optimal policy
Owner files: `sim/loop/` (new package), `sim/policies/loop_*.yaml`, `tests/test_loop*.py`, `docs/loop.md`, `docs/img/loop-*.png`, `docs/status/trackD.md`, `docs/assumptions-trackD.md`. You branch from Track A's M4 code: use its policy transforms, baselines (10 % weekday, `runs/`), matched-control machinery and `baseline_launch`; do not edit Track A's files except one appended registration line if a transform registry needs it. Machine rules: run at most ONE city-scale run at a time, routing threads 4, gzip outputs (Track A's launcher does this), and check `df -h /System/Volumes/Data` before each run: if free space is under 3 GB, stop and write `LOOP: blocked - disk`.

Goal: model Boring Company style "Loop" tunnels in Jeddah (small-bore 3.6 m tunnels, station-to-station EV shuttles, surface stations), find the best corridors, and find the policy that gives the most congestion relief per riyal. Everything is synthetic and uncalibrated; say so in every output.

Facts to ground the assumptions (register each with its source via the `assumptions-register` skill; use `data-scout` for anything else):
- Dubai Loop (RTA contract, Feb 2026): pilot 6.4 km, 4 stations, DIFC to Dubai Mall, about AED 565 M; full 22.2 km, 19 stations, about AED 2 B; 3.6 m tunnels; speeds "up to 100 mph" claimed.
- Vegas Loop: operating speed 35 mph (56 km/h) per Wikipedia infobox, 2026 reporting says no more than 30 mph; measured peak 1,355 passengers/hour (July 2021) vs a 4,400/hour test and a 90,000/hour company claim for full build-out; Tesla Model Y with human drivers; typical waits 10-15 min; LVCC Loop cost $53 M for 1.7-2.2 miles. Music City Loop about $240 M for ~13 miles (~$18.5 M/mile). Use a cost range of USD 15-40 M per km (document) and a capacity range from measured (1,355 pph per station pair) to engineering (1-2 s headways, 1.5-3 pax per vehicle).

Model (in SUMO, all parameters in YAML):
1. **Tunnel network**: for a candidate alignment (ordered station list of lon/lat), add one tunnel edge per direction between consecutive stations with `vClass` restricted to `loop_shuttle` (shuttle mode) or `passenger` with a toll (private-EV mode), speed from a sweep {50, 90, 160 km/h}, one lane, headway via vType `minGap`/`tau`; a station edge with a 20-40 s stop for loading; connections to the surface network at the station's nearest main-SCC edge. Write the modified network under a new name (`data/jeddah/jeddah.loop-<id>.net.xml.gz`), never the base network.
2. **Shuttle mode (policy `loop_shuttle`)**: trips whose origin is within R_o and destination within R_d of two different stations shift to the Loop with share α (sweep α ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7}, R ∈ {800, 1500} m); their car trips are removed and replaced by shuttle vehicle flows between stations (pax / occupancy, plus an empty-repositioning factor); report the share of shifted trips that exceed the station-pair capacity (queue) as a quality flag.
3. **Private-EV mode (policy `loop_private`)**: tunnel open to passenger cars with a toll (edge price via routing weights) and the headway capacity limit; cars re-route through it if faster. Run with the matched control (Track A's TA-018) because this re-routes baseline vehicles.
4. **Siting**: from the 10 % weekday baseline, compute district-to-district desire lines weighted by vehicle trips × baseline delay per trip, and the top-delay corridors from edge data; generate 6-8 candidate alignments (station spacing 1.5-3 km, 4-8 stations, 6-25 km each), anchored where the data says, with KAIA airport, Al Balad/Corniche, Tahlia/Al Andalus and KAU as natural anchors to test but not to assume; draw them on a map with district outlines (`docs/img/loop-candidates.png`).
5. **Evaluation**: each candidate as a policy run at 10 % (shuttle mode, default α 0.5, speed 90) against the matched baseline; KPIs: city VHT saved, AM-peak delay change, passengers served, tunnel-km, capex range, VHT saved per km and per million USD; noise band from Track A's replicate machinery.
6. **Optimal policy**: greedy selection of corridors under a 25 km budget, then a sweep over mode (shuttle vs private-EV), α, speed and toll on the selected set; recommend the configuration with the best VHT saved per million USD, with the uncertainty band and the quality flags (teleport share, capacity exceedance). State clearly which part of the result is driven by vendor claims (speed, capacity) versus measured Vegas figures.
7. `python -m sim.loop.site` (candidates + map), `python -m sim.loop.evaluate --candidates ...` (runs), `python -m sim.loop.optimise` (greedy + sweep, writes `docs/loop.md` results tables); tests on the demo window; demo YAML `sim/policies/loop_demo.yaml` that runs on the demo window in seconds.
Done when: the candidate map, the ranking table, the recommended corridors and policy, and the caveats are in `docs/loop.md`, tests pass, verifier passes. Write `LOOP: done`.
