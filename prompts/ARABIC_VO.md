# Pass 8 — Arabic voice-over edition of every film (ARABIC_VO)

Owner's request (verbatim): "create an arabic voice over in another set of videos. i send arabic ones to arabic speakers and english ones to english speakers". You are in the main checkout on `main` (HEAD 43546ee+). Read `docs/movies.md` and `scripts/movies/narrate.py` first: the English pipeline is narration YAML (`movies/specs/<film>.narration.yaml`) -> Kokoro-82M via `scripts/movies/tts_kokoro.py` in `.venv-tts` (torch 2.14.1, MPS available) -> timing -> voice track (-16 LUFS) -> mux + mov_text subtitles + .srt. Kokoro has no Arabic. Build the Arabic edition as a second language of the same pipeline, not a fork. Keep the English films exactly as they are.

## Part 1 — choose the Arabic TTS (offline, open weights; no cloud TTS)
Evaluate at most three candidates on the Mac, in a separate venv `.venv-tts-ar` (do not touch `.venv-tts`): (a) **Chatterbox Multilingual** (Resemble AI, MIT, Arabic supported; `chatterbox-tts` on PyPI, model `ResembleAI/chatterbox`), the preferred candidate on licence + quality; (b) **Fish Audio OpenAudio S1-mini** (Arabic supported; check its licence — CC-BY-NC-SA would be documented as non-commercial for the voice track only); (c) **Coqui XTTS-v2** (Arabic, CPML non-commercial) or **Piper `ar_JO-kareem`** (MIT, fast, robotic) as fallbacks. Pick with these checks on three test lines (one short, one with numbers, one long): runs on this Mac (MPS or CPU) in reasonable time per line; output 24-48 kHz WAV with no silence gaps or truncation; speech rate 2.5-4 Arabic syllables/s; the model must read **fully vowelised Modern Standard Arabic** well (use `pyarabic`/`mishkal` or write the tashkeel by hand in the YAML for the numbers and proper nouns). Write the samples to `runs/movies/_voice_ar/samples/<model>-<n>.wav` and a comparison note in `docs/movies-arabic.md` (licence, speed, what you heard in the waveform/spectrogram checks: duration vs expected, clipping, silence). Decide and state the choice as RA-001 in that doc. If none works, stop with `ARABIC_VO: blocked — <reason>`.

## Part 2 — Arabic narration scripts
For every film with a narration YAML (city-24h, tahlia-street, incident, events, strategist, evacuation, policy-lab, loop, obhur-bridge, showreel): write `movies/specs/<film>.narration.ar.yaml` with the same `shot`/`at` anchors and a line-by-line **Modern Standard Arabic** (الفصحى, broadcast/documentary register, not dialect) translation of the English text. Rules: numbers written in Arabic words where the TTS mis-reads digits (test first), otherwise Arabic-Indic or Western digits consistently; keep every figure identical to the English file (the `narrate` check against the docs must still pass: extend `check()` to compare the digits of the Arabic line against its English counterpart rather than the docs); place names in their Arabic forms (شرم أبحر، طريق الملك عبدالعزيز، طريق التحلية، جامعة الملك عبدالعزيز، الكورنيش الشمالي، طريق المدينة المنورة); "synthetic demand, uncalibrated" -> «طلب اصطناعي، غير معاير» on every film's closing line as in English; no letter-spacing anywhere. Keep lines short enough that the Arabic track fits the same shots (Arabic runs ~15-20 % longer; the timing planner may stretch shots, keep each film within +25 % of its English length).
